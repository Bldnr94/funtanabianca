The Marchetti Family

Two generations
in the heart of Corsica

Marina grew up here, among the chestnut trees planted by her grandfather and the goat's cheeses aged by her mother. At 1,300 metres above sea level, at the end of a dirt track winding up from Quenza, Funtana Bianca is not a hotel — it is a family farm open to travellers who seek the real thing.

Every dish comes from the estate's organic farm. The Nustrale charcuterie, the Tra li Monti cheeses, the jams, the bread — everything is homemade or produced metres from your table. Marina, her mother and her husband cook together as a family, as they always have.

"Here, we don't play at being authentic. We simply are."
Marina Marchetti Funtana Bianca · Quenza

How to find us

Getting
here

The inn is 6 km from Quenza, at 1,300 m altitude and 5 km from the Cuscionu plateau. It is reached via a small mountain road.

01

Leave from the centre of Quenza towards the Cuscionu plateau.

02

After about 5.5 km, you will see the Pierrot stables on your left. Pass them.

03

500 m past the stables, turn left: a short dirt track leads you straight to the inn.

04

Total from Quenza: about 6 km. Allow 15 minutes.

Frequently asked questions

Everything to know
before you visit

How do I confirm a booking?

Bookings are by phone only: +33 6 81 17 18 17 or +33 7 62 60 92 91. A bank transfer deposit confirms the reservation. The balance is paid in cash on arrival.

What is the cancellation policy?

Please let us know as early as possible if you need to cancel. For private events, a minimum notice period applies — contact us directly for the specific conditions.

Are pets welcome?

Pets are welcome on a lead at the restaurant (animals roam freely on the estate). However, pets are not permitted inside the Caseddu rooms.

What is included in a Caseddu stay?

Each Caseddu has a 160 cm + 90 cm bed, a private bathroom (WC, basin, shower), gas heating, and bed linen and towels are provided. Breakfast is extra (€10 / person). Check-in from 4 pm, check-out by 10 am.

Can I order charcuterie to take home?

Yes — our Nustrale charcuterie and Tra li Monti Marchetti cheeses are available to order, with delivery across Corsica and mainland France. Call us to place an order.

Is the inn accessible by standard car?

Yes. The track leading to the inn from Quenza is passable in a standard vehicle. Allow about 6 km from Quenza centre and 15 minutes of mountain road.
